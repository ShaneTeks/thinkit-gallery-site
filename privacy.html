The short version

  • Think'It Gallery has no ads and works offline. It organises, searches and edits the photos, videos, audio and documents on your phone, and all processing happens on your phone.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers that receive your files, searches, or usage. The only personal data we ever receive is what you send us if you email us (see section 5).
  • Nothing goes to the cloud. Every smart feature (search, voice search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, People) runs on your phone. The models are optional downloads you choose; the hold-to-cut-out model is built in.
  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. The Google Play version is free with a 7-day full trial, then has one optional in-app purchase, "Unlock full app", handled entirely by Google Play (the app only checks whether you own it; we never see payment details). The app never syncs your files with any server.
  • The app connects to the internet only to download the models you choose (search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, people; each once) and, in the direct-download version, to check for and download app updates. The Google Play version also contacts Google Play to buy and restore "Unlock full app".

Face data (People)

People is optional and off until you download its small face model and name someone.

  • On this phone only. Faces are found and matched on your phone. Think'It never sends your photos or face data anywhere; we never receive it.
  • Names are yours. A name exists only because you typed it. The app never suggests or looks up who someone is.
  • Your choice: nothing is scanned for faces until you turn People on, download its model and confirm. Only turn it on for photos of people you are allowed to organise this way.
  • What is stored: for each photo looked at, the position of each face and a list of numbers describing it (a "face embedding"), plus the names you created. It is kept in the app's private storage and excluded from Android backups and device transfers.
  • Delete anytime: Settings › AI features › People lets you rename or delete a person (their face data goes too) or delete all face data at once. Uninstalling the app deletes it as well. Turning People off stops new face scanning. Your photos are never changed.
  • How long it is kept: face data stays on your phone only until you delete it or uninstall the app. We never hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or destroy.
  • No sharing: face data is never shared, sold or used for anything else. The app connects to the internet only to download the models you choose (and, in the direct-download version, to check for updates).

2. Network connections

The app uses the internet only for the downloads below. Everything else (search, voice search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, People, photos-to-video, editing, the equalizer and playback) works offline.

1. Model downloads — when you choose a model, the app downloads its files (about 5–500 MB) from our public GitHub Releases page (github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.). If a file isn't available there, the app downloads the identical file from the original publisher's page instead: transcript models from the whisper.cpp project on Hugging Face (huggingface.co, Hugging Face, Inc.), text-reading models from the RapidOCR project on ModelScope (modelscope.cn, Alibaba Group), cut-out models from the rembg project on GitHub and the MobileSAM files on Hugging Face, face models from the OpenCV Zoo project on Hugging Face. The app checks every file's SHA-256 fingerprint before use. As with any download, the host receives your IP address and standard request headers; see its privacy statement. No information about your files, searches, transcripts or photos is sent. Transcribing, reading text and cut-outs run entirely on your phone.
2. App updates (direct-download version only) — the app fetches a small update file from GitHub (when you open it, at most twice a day; once a day in the background if "Notify me of updates" is on; and when you tap Check for updates) and, if you agree, downloads the new version. Test versions given to invited testers may check a private test channel of ours first; only the same small update file and the app download are fetched, and nothing about you or your files is sent. The Google Play version never downloads or installs app files itself: it is updated only through Google Play.
3. Purchases (Google Play version only) — to buy "Unlock full app" and to restore it (for example after reinstalling or on a new phone), the app contacts Google Play through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details, and the app only learns whether you own the unlock. Google may share basic order details with us (such as the order number, country and amount) through the Google Play Console; we use them only to handle refunds, accounting and tax, and keep them as long as tax law requires.
4. Sharing — when you share a file, Android sends it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy applies.

3. Permissions

Photos-to-video and clip sharing are made on your phone with FFmpeg (an open-source video library bundled in the app); nothing is uploaded. Background playback keeps music playing after you leave the player, with a media notification you can swipe away when paused; the equalizer uses Android's built-in audio effect on your phone.

Battery: indexing new files for search (and reading text, transcripts or faces, if you turn those on) runs in the background only within Android's limits; by default it waits until the phone is charging or has enough battery, and stops when the battery is low. Optional automatic model downloads (off by default) run only on Wi-Fi while charging. Battery saver also turns off decorative animations.

Photos and videos / Music and audio / (optionally) chosen document folders, Notifications (progress of indexing and new-media syncing), Set wallpaper, Modify system settings (only when you choose "Set as ringtone"; you grant it in Android's settings), Foreground service (see below), Run at startup / background work (to index new media while the app is closed, if you enable auto-sync), Internet (the downloads above only), Microphone (only while you use voice search after tapping the mic: on Android 12+ speech is recognised by your phone's on-device recogniser; on older phones the app asks Android's speech recogniser for offline mode, but if your phone's recogniser cannot work offline it may use its own online service under its provider's privacy policy (for example Google). Think'It records nothing, stores no audio and sends nothing to us).

Foreground services run only with a visible notification: data sync while a model download or library indexing that you started is in progress, and media playback while music keeps playing after you leave the player. The direct-download version may also request "Install unknown apps" (updates) and "All files access" (documents). You can revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature then stops working, the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Children

Think'It Gallery is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone. Where local law requires a parent's permission for a child to use apps or make purchases, Google Play's family settings apply.

5. Your rights (worldwide)

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no personal data from the app about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. You control everything on your device: delete files in the app, delete face data in Settings › AI features › People, clear app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove the index, models and settings.

If you email us: we receive your email address, name (if given) and what you write. We use it only to answer you, which is in our legitimate interest and yours, and delete it within 12 months after the matter is closed unless we need it for a legal claim or a refund record. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). You can ask us to see, correct or delete it, or object to our use of it, by writing to the same address. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Information Regulator in South Africa (inforegulator.org.za), your EU national authority, or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

How this fits the main privacy laws:

  • Namibia — Article 13 of the Namibian Constitution protects your privacy. As of October 2026 Namibia has no data protection act in force and no data protection authority; we will update this policy if one is enacted.
  • EU/UK GDPR and South Africa's POPIA — the app processes your files, faces and other data only on your phone, under your control, and never sends them to us; we do not receive or control that data. The emails you send us are the only personal data we handle, as explained above.
  • United States (including California and state biometric privacy laws such as Illinois BIPA) — we do not collect, receive, store, sell or share personal information or biometric data. Face data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you delete it or uninstall.

6. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox and your screen lock. Downloads are fetched over HTTPS and integrity-checked (SHA-256).
