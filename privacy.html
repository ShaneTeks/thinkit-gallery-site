Applies to: the Think'It Gallery app for Android (Google Play and direct-download versions). The app was renamed to Think'It Gallery in October 2026; its Android package names did not change, and nothing about how your data is handled changed with the name.

The short version

  • Think'It Gallery has no ads and works offline. It organises, searches and edits the photos, videos, audio and documents on your phone, and all processing happens on your phone.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers that receive your files, searches, or usage.
  • No cloud AI. Every AI feature (search, voice search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, People) runs on your phone. The AI models are optional downloads you choose; the hold-to-cut-out model is built in.
  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. The Google Play version is free with a 7-day full trial, then has one optional in-app purchase, "Unlock full app", handled entirely by Google Play (the app only checks whether you own it; we never see payment details). The app never syncs your files with any server.
  • The app connects to the internet only to download the AI models you choose (search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, people; each once) and, in the direct-download version, to check for and download app updates.

Face data (People)

People is optional and off until you download its small face model and name someone.

  • On this phone only. Faces are found and matched on your phone. Think'It never sends your photos or face data anywhere; we never receive it.
  • Names are yours. A name exists only because you typed it. The app never suggests or looks up who someone is.
  • What is stored: for each photo looked at, the position of each face and a list of numbers describing it (a "face embedding"), plus the names you created. It is kept in the app's private storage and excluded from Android backups and device transfers.
  • Delete anytime: Settings › AI features › People lets you rename or delete a person (their face data goes too) or delete all face data at once. Uninstalling the app deletes it as well. Your photos are never changed.
  • No sharing: face data is never shared, sold or used for anything else. The app connects to the internet only to download the models you choose (and, in the direct-download version, to check for updates).

2. Network connections

The app uses the internet only for the downloads below. Everything else (search, voice search, transcripts, text in photos, cut-outs, People, photos-to-video, editing, the equalizer and playback) works offline.

1. AI model downloads — when you choose a model, the app downloads its files (about 5–500 MB) from our public GitHub Releases page (github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.). If a file isn't available there, the app downloads the identical file from the original publisher's page instead: transcript models from the whisper.cpp project on Hugging Face (huggingface.co, Hugging Face, Inc.), text-reading models from the RapidOCR project on ModelScope (modelscope.cn, Alibaba Group), cut-out models from the rembg project on GitHub and the MobileSAM files on Hugging Face, face models from the OpenCV Zoo project on Hugging Face. The app checks every file's SHA-256 fingerprint before use. As with any download, the host receives your IP address and standard request headers; see its privacy statement. No information about your files, searches, transcripts or photos is sent. Transcribing, reading text and cut-outs run entirely on your phone.
2. App updates (direct-download version only) — the app fetches a small update file from GitHub (when you open it, at most twice a day; once a day in the background if "Notify me of updates" is on; and when you tap Check for updates) and, if you agree, downloads the new version. Test builds given to invited testers may first check a private GitHub repository of ours (using a read-only access key built into that test version) and fall back to the public page; only the same small update file and the app download are fetched, and nothing about you or your files is sent. The Google Play version never downloads or installs app files itself: it is updated only through Google Play.
3. Sharing — when you share a file, Android sends it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy applies.

3. Permissions

Photos-to-video and clip sharing are made on your phone with FFmpeg (an open-source video library bundled in the app); nothing is uploaded. Background playback keeps music playing after you leave the player, with a media notification you can swipe away when paused; the equalizer uses Android's built-in audio effect on your phone.

Battery: indexing new files for search (and reading text, transcripts or faces, if you turn those on) runs in the background only within Android's limits; by default it waits until the phone is charging or has enough battery, and stops when the battery is low. Optional automatic model downloads (off by default) run only on Wi-Fi while charging. Battery saver also turns off decorative animations.

Photos and videos / Music and audio / (optionally) chosen document folders, Notifications (progress of indexing and new-media syncing), Set wallpaper, Modify system settings (only when you choose "Set as ringtone"; you grant it in Android's settings), Foreground service (see below), Run at startup / background work (to index new media while the app is closed, if you enable auto-sync), Internet (the downloads above only), Microphone (only while you use voice search after tapping the mic: on Android 12+ speech is recognised by your phone's on-device recogniser; on older phones the app asks Android's recogniser for offline mode. The app records nothing, stores no audio and sends nothing).

Foreground services run only with a visible notification: data sync while an AI model download or library indexing that you started is in progress, and media playback while music keeps playing after you leave the player. The direct-download version may also request "Install unknown apps" (updates) and "All files access" (documents). You can revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature then stops working, the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Children

Think'It Gallery is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone.

5. Your rights (worldwide)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on our side. You control everything on your device: delete files in the app, clear app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove the index, models and settings. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply and delete it when the matter is closed.

This approach is designed to be consistent with privacy principles under:

  • Namibia — Article 13 of the Namibian Constitution (right to privacy). As of October 2026 Namibia's Data Protection Bill has not been enacted and there is no data-protection authority; we will update this policy if it becomes law.
  • EU/UK GDPR — no personal data is processed by us as controller; on-device processing happens under your control.
  • South Africa POPIA, California CCPA/CPRA and similar laws — we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

6. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox and your screen lock. Downloads are fetched over HTTPS and integrity-checked (SHA-256).
