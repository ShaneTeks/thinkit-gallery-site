How it works

Three steps, once. After that, search is instant.

Install and pick a model

Choose Fast, Balanced or Best. We recommend the one your phone runs well. It downloads once, then works offline.

Your phone learns your library

ThinkIt Gallery looks at each photo, video and recording on the device and builds a private index. New files are added automatically.

Describe what you remember

Type a few words. The best matches appear right above your thumb, with photos, videos and audio together.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no account.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Indexing and search run entirely on your phone. Transcripts and text in photos are made on the phone too. The app only uses the internet to download the AI models you choose (once each) and, in the direct-download version, to check for app updates. See the Privacy Policy.

How much space does the AI model need?

Fast is about 157 MB and searches names, dates, tags and document text. Balanced is about 370 MB and also understands photos and video frames. Best is about 462 MB and also understands sound in videos and audio recordings. You can keep one or several, switch or delete them any time in Settings › AI models. The gallery also works without a model.

Will it drain my battery?

The first index of a large library takes a while and uses power, so you can let it run only while charging. After that only new files are indexed, a few seconds each.

Can it transcribe voice notes?

Yes. Download a transcript model in Settings › AI models (Fast about 42 MB, Balanced about 57 MB, Accurate about 181 MB), open a recording or video and tap Transcript. It detects the language, or you pick it. You can also let new voice notes be transcribed in the background while charging. Transcripts stay on your phone and are searchable.

Which phones are supported?

Any Android 7.0 or newer. AI search needs a 64-bit phone (almost every phone since 2017); on older 32-bit phones the gallery, editing and everything else still work.

What if I replace a photo by mistake?

When you save an edit over the original, the original is kept for 24 hours. Tap Undo right away, or restore it later from Settings › Recently replaced.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, search, transcripts and text in photos work in airplane mode.

Does it work on tablets?

It runs on Android tablets and foldables, but it is designed and tested for phones first.
